# Executive Summary

**Product:** Credit Risk – Roll Matrix Deep Analysis  
**Version:** 1.0

This demo presents a production‑grade Power BI solution for monitoring portfolio health, analysing vintage lifecycles and running roll‑rate based stress scenarios. The platform consolidates exposure, delinquency migration and scenario outputs into governance‑ready dashboards that support IFRS‑9 aligned decision making.

Key benefits
- **Fast insight:** Executive dashboard surfaces top KPIs (Exposure, NPL, PCR, EL) and directional trends for immediate management review.  
- **Early warning:** Roll‑rate matrices and inflow metrics detect early deterioration and quantify cure vs deterioration dynamics.  
- **Scenario‑driven:** Scenario engine translates migration shocks into Expected Loss, NPL and Cost of Risk forecasts for capital and provisioning planning.  
- **Cohort analytics:** Vintage lifecycle views enable cohort comparison, CDR heatmaps and run‑off attribution for origination quality assessment.

Intended users
- Risk management leads, IFRS‑9 model owners, portfolio analytics teams and senior management requiring concise, auditable risk metrics and scenario outputs.

Delivery model
- Public demo includes screenshots and a preview PDF. The full commercial package (sanitised PBIX, deployment docs, commercial license) is delivered to paying customers with a license and support package.
